# Plumb: positioning, offer and pricing

*Draft, 21 Nov 2026. Built from the brand guide and the hybrid UI mockups. Anything marked **Open** still needs a decision.*

## 1. One line

Plumb is an AI-native analyst firm that shows a brand how its marketing stacks up in its market, and what it takes to improve.

**Tagline:** Know what good looks like.

## 2. The problem

Marketing leaders can't see where they stand.

- **Shopping is moving to places they can't measure.** AI assistants, AI search and shopping agents now decide which brands a buyer sees. Most brands don't know if they are named, recommended or purchasable there.
- **Spend is not the same as presence.** A brand can be a top spender and still be absent from AI answers. Paid presence hides weak earned presence.
- **No honest benchmark for the work itself.** Brands can't tell whether their agencies and in-house teams are fast, cheap and good compared with peers doing the same job.
- **AI changes how the work gets done, but nobody shows the receipts.** Teams hear that AI workflows cut cost and time, but see no measured, comparable proof.

Traditional analyst firms publish opinion, slowly. Agencies grade their own homework. Dashboards show your data, not the market's.

## 3. What Plumb is

An analyst firm built on measurement rather than opinion, run with AI so it can measure every brand in a category every month.

| Part | What it is | What the customer gets |
|---|---|---|
| **The Plumb Index** (data product) | Monthly category rankings across narrative share, share of spend, AI answer share, search share, agent readiness, and bought vs earned. | Their rank, their gap to #1, and why. |
| **Peer boards** | Closed boards of about 12 comparable brands in a vertical. Members share benchmarks and compare workflows. | A trusted peer set to compare cost, speed and quality with, beyond the public Index. |
| **AI-native playbooks** | Forkable workflows (team, stack, prompts, review gates, oversight) with outcomes Plumb re-measures each time someone runs them. | A proven way to close a specific gap, and evidence that it works. |

The loop: **measure** (Index) → **compare** (boards) → **improve** (playbooks) → **re-measure** (Index).

## 4. Who it's for

**Primary buyer:** the CMO or VP Marketing at a consumer brand with roughly $50M to $1B in revenue, who uses outside agencies and is under pressure to show what AI changes.

**Also:** heads of growth, ecommerce and brand; marketing ops leads who own the stack and agency roster.

**First category:** Beauty (the first Index edition). Next candidates shown in the report signup: Apparel, Footwear, Home, Food & bev.

**Open:** which second vertical, and whether retail (as in the dashboard mockup) is a category or a cross-category board.

## 5. What we measure

Every score is published with its method beside it.

| Vector | Method |
|---|---|
| Narrative share | Share of brand mentions across category narratives in AI answers, search and ad copy. |
| Share of spend | Share of active ads and new creatives (proxy until spend data is licensed). |
| AI answer share | Share of about 200 category prompts that name the brand, across 4 AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode). |
| Search share | Share of category search visibility. **Open:** method. |
| Agent readiness | Can a shopping agent find, compare and add to cart. Scripted, monthly. Pass / Partial / Fail. |
| Bought vs earned | Share of spend compared with narrative, AI and search share. High spend with low AI share means presence is paid for. |

For members, Plumb also reads the work itself: **cost per asset, review rounds, time per asset and panel quality**, benchmarked by vertical, brand vs direct response, channel and AI role. Agencies are scored against every agency in the Index doing the same work, and are never told unless the brand invites them.

## 6. The offer

| | **Benchmark** | **Membership** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | $500 / month |
| Who | Any brand, from just a website | Brands ready to act on the gap |
| Includes | Readings on cost, speed and review rounds · your rank and gap to #1 · the public Index and playbooks · the monthly report | Full analysis on every vector (vertical, brand vs DR, media, channel, stage, AI role) · market scan: narrative, brand, CX and AI commerce rank in your vertical · your agencies benchmarked against peers · a forkable playbook to close your gap · a seat on a peer board |
| CTA | Measure me → | Become a member |

**Also offered:**
- **Monthly report** (free, per category): who moved, who is buying their presence, what leaders do differently. Email, RSS and API.
- **Implementers:** a forward-deployed Plumb creative stands up a playbook's team, stack and oversight with the brand. **Open:** priced per engagement or included in a higher tier.
- **API and MCP access** to Index data, so the data is built for people and AI to read and cite. **Open:** pricing.

**Open on pricing:**
- $500/month looks low for agency benchmarking plus a peer board seat. Consider a higher board tier, or pricing by brand revenue band.
- Whether a peer board seat is part of Membership or a separate, higher tier.
- Annual vs monthly billing.

## 7. Why Plumb wins

- **Rankings can't be bought.** Plumb takes no money from the brands it ranks for placement, and shows its method beside every score.
- **Measured, not opinion.** Every claim is a number with a method and a sample size.
- **AI-native means coverage.** Plumb listens like a customer (subscribes, follows, prompts AI engines) across every brand in a category, every month, at a cost a human analyst firm can't match.
- **From diagnosis to fix.** The Index says where you stand. Playbooks say what to do, and show who has already done it.
- **A network effect.** Each brand that benchmarks or forks a playbook adds data, which makes the next benchmark sharper.

## 8. How a customer gets started

1. Enter a website, get a free score and rank.
2. Onboard in about 10 minutes: business, stack, agencies, listening address and channels, media plan.
3. First full rankings in about 14 days.
4. Join a peer board, fork a playbook, re-benchmark monthly.

## 9. Before anything goes public

- The mockups use real brand names (Glossier, Rare Beauty, CeraVe and others) with invented scores. Replace them with real Index data or fictional brands.
- Write down the Index method in enough detail to defend each ranking.
- Decide how share of spend is sourced (proxy vs licensed).

## 10. Open questions

1. How peer boards are formed and run: who selects members, how often they meet, what is shared and under what confidentiality.
2. Who pays: the brand only, or also agencies (to be benchmarked or listed)? Paid agency listings would conflict with "rankings can't be bought".
3. Playbook licensing ("Plumb Open Playbook") and whether outside authors are paid.
4. Second category and launch timing.
5. Pricing questions in section 6.
