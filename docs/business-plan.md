# Plumb business plan (one page)

> **Status: draft.** Builds on the [positioning brief](positioning-brief.md) (PR #1). The only settled decisions are from the Oct 12 strategy session: Plumb is an MSP moving up the stack, not an agency; it replaces internal engineering and SaaS procurement; retainers are the goal. Every number below is a working assumption marked *(to validate)*.

**Plumb builds and runs the software your business depends on, for a monthly fee.**

## Problem
Operations-heavy businesses run on a pile of SaaS seats, spreadsheets, automations and the one person who knows how it all fits together. Software spend keeps rising, the tools never quite fit, and when that person leaves, nobody owns the system. Hiring an engineering team is too expensive and too risky at their size; agencies build something, hand it off, and walk away.

## Solution
A managed software service. Plumb audits the client's tools and spend, builds custom internal tools and integrations with AI-assisted development, then hosts, monitors, fixes and improves them every month. The client owns the code and data. Plumb owns keeping it working.

## Customer
- **Company:** too big for duct tape, too small for an engineering team *(to validate: 20 to 500 employees)*. First vertical still open.
- **Buyer:** the COO, CFO, head of operations or founder, the same person who signs the IT/MSP contract.
- **Triggers:** a big SaaS renewal, the in-house builder leaving, a process outgrowing no-code, or leadership asking why software spend keeps rising.

## Business model
| Revenue line | Shape | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Fixed fee: audit, migration, first build | *(to validate)* |
| Retainer: *Run* | Maintain what exists | *(to validate)* / month |
| Retainer: *Run + Build* | Maintenance plus monthly build capacity | *(to validate)* / month |
| Retainer: *Embedded* | A dedicated squad | *(to validate)* / month |

No standalone projects. Annual terms with a prepay discount. Priced against what it replaces (a fully loaded engineer plus cancelled SaaS), not against agency day rates.

**Why the economics work:** AI-assisted building lowers the cost of each build, and running many similar systems on a shared platform lowers the cost of each client. Margin should improve as clients and systems under management grow *(to validate: target gross margin, delivery hours per client per month)*.

## Go to market
1. **Founder-led sales** into the operator network: start with 3 to 5 design-partner clients at a reduced rate in exchange for case studies *(to validate: count and discount)*.
2. **Lead with the audit:** a SaaS-spend and workflow audit is the door opener and turns into the onboarding fee.
3. **Proof:** publish SaaS spend removed, hours saved and systems under management per client.
4. **Partners:** test referral or resale through traditional IT MSPs who already own the buyer relationship *(open question)*.

## Competition and edge
- **Agencies and freelancers:** project-based, leave after launch. Plumb's product is the ongoing ownership.
- **Hiring in-house:** slow, costly, single point of failure. Plumb is one retainer with no hiring risk.
- **More SaaS / no-code:** seat sprawl and workflow compromise. Plumb consolidates and fits the tool to the business.
- **IT MSPs:** trusted by the same buyer, but stop at infrastructure. Plumb moves up the stack.

## Milestones *(to validate: dates and targets)*
| Stage | Goal |
|---|---|
| Next 90 days | Pick first vertical, set price points, sign first design partners |
| First year | Convert design partners to full retainers, first referenceable case studies, repeatable onboarding playbook |
| After that | Standardise the platform, hire delivery, add the *Embedded* tier |

## Key metrics
Monthly recurring revenue, number of retainer clients, systems under management, gross margin per client, net revenue retention, logo churn, and SaaS spend removed for clients.

## Risks
- **Scope creep** turns retainers into unpaid project work. Mitigation: capacity-based tiers with clear limits.
- **Founder bottleneck** in delivery. Mitigation: shared platform, playbooks and AI tooling before hiring.
- **Support liability** for systems Plumb runs. Mitigation: defined response times, monitoring, standard stack.
- **Category confusion** with agencies. Mitigation: MSP language and ops/finance buyer, per the brief.

## Open decisions
First vertical, actual price points and tier limits, minimum contract length, the IT MSP partner question, and how much funding (if any) is needed before the first retainers cover costs *(to validate)*.
