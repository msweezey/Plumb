Method

Proof, not promises. Scores can't be bought.

Each task is a real workflow that crosses several tools. We run it in sandbox accounts, at each level, with several AI models, in each vertical. Every run records time, cost, tool calls, human interventions and safety. Practitioner boards choose the tasks and grade the final outcome.

The winning run becomes a Plumb Line: a playbook your team or your agents can follow, fork and grade. Tools show up inside the workflows they serve, so vendors see exactly where they help and where they break.
