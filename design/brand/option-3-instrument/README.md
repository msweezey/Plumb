# Plumb — Option 3

**Know what good looks like.**

Option 3, *Instrument*, treats Plumb as a precision measuring tool. Pure white, graphite type, hairlines, ruler ticks and one vivid **level green**, the color of a spirit-level bubble sitting dead center. Where Option 1 is friendly SaaS and Option 2 is a loud magazine, Option 3 is calm, exact and trustworthy: the brand you'd want holding the ruler.

## Principles

- **Measure, don't decorate.** Every visual element is a reading, a scale or a rule.
- **White and graphite.** Content sits on white with hairlines; color is earned.
- **Green means true.** Level green marks what meets the standard: grade A, the gauge marker, your gains. Amber means off-level.
- **Readings, not charts.** Scores are shown as gauges on tick scales, like an instrument face.
- **Quietly confident.** Sentence-case headlines, tight tracking, no shouting.

## Logo

A **spirit level**: a capsule with two ticks and a green bubble dead center, next to a lowercase monoline *plumb*. The level alone is the app icon. The **Plumbed seal** is a dial with 60 tick marks around the level.

## Type

- **Inter Tight** 600 for headlines, tight tracking.
- **Inter** for text.
- **JetBrains Mono** for readings and labels.

## Signature components

- **Level gauge:** a tick scale with a marker; the green zone shows "good". Used for cost, speed, rounds and quality.
- **Ruler divider:** a row of ticks with a longer tick every fifth, opening each section.
- **Level badge:** the grade inside a capsule; A is filled green.
