# Plumb — Option 2

**Know what good looks like.**

Option 2 is the editorial direction: Plumb as a magazine that publishes benchmarks. Stark bone and ink, one hot red, huge condensed type, hard rules instead of cards. Where Option 1 (purple) feels like a friendly SaaS product, Option 2 feels like an authority with an opinion: closer to A24 posters, Supreme and a financial paper than to a dashboard.

## Principles

- **Say it big.** One giant claim per page, set in condensed capitals.
- **Rules, not boxes.** Content is organized by heavy and hairline rules. No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners.
- **One red thing.** Signal red marks the single most important thing in view: the #1, the headline number, the plumb line.
- **Numbers are the art.** Data is set large in mono and allowed to dominate.
- **Black and bone only.** Everything else is ink on bone or bone on ink.

## Logo

The wordmark is **PLUMB** drawn as a custom block-letter condensed face, with a red **plumb line**: a thin vertical rule with a square bob hanging off the B. The mark alone (line and bob on a black square) is the app icon and favicon.

- Wordmark in ink on bone, or bone on ink. The plumb line is always signal red.
- The **Plumbed seal** is a black square with the line, PLUMBED and the grade and year.
- Never round the corners, outline the letters or put the wordmark on red.

## Color

| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| `bone` | Every page background |
| `ink` | Text, rules, black blocks, the wordmark |
| `lead` | Secondary text |
| `signal` | One red thing per view; large text or fills only |
| `signal-ink` | Red as small text and links |
| `rule-soft` | Hairlines between rows |
| `pass` | Meets the standard, always with a word |

Bone on ink is used for full-bleed black bands: the stat ticker, the benchmark block, the footer.

## Type

- **Archivo Narrow** (700, all caps) for headlines, ranks and covers. Tight leading (0.86–0.92).
- **Archivo** for running text.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for numbers, labels and kickers. All caps for labels.

## Layout

- 12-column grid, 48px margins, sections 96px apart.
- An 8px ink bar opens the hero and the leaderboard; 2px ink rules open sections; 1px `rule-soft` divides rows.
- Square corners everywhere (`radius-0`).
- Tables are full-width, with rank numbers set large in the display face.

## Voice

Same as Option 1: irreverent, but every line backed by data. Option 2 lets the headlines be louder: "WHO'S ACTUALLY GOOD AT AI CONTENT." Then the numbers prove it.
