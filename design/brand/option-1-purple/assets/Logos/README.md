# Logos

- **plumb-lockup.svg** is the bob and wordmark, the default logo. Wordmark and string in Plumb purple `#6A2BE0`, bob in light purple `#9B6BFF`, for white or paper grounds.
- **plumb-wordmark.svg** is the wordmark alone in Plumb purple, for white or paper grounds.
- **plumb-wordmark-reversed.svg** is the wordmark in white, for purple or ink grounds.
- **plumb-mark.svg** is the bob alone, for favicons, avatars and app icons.
- **plumbed-badge.svg** is the Plumbed seal awarded with a passing eval: a deep purple `#1E1238` disc. Update the grade and year per award.

Clear space equals the bob's height. Minimum wordmark width is 64px on screen.
