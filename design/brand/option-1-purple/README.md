# Plumb

**Know what good looks like.**

Plumb is an AI-native analyst firm for marketing. We run evals on real marketing workflows, publish the playbooks that win, and set the standard that agencies, brands and vendors are judged against. A plumb line is the true vertical every builder checks their work against. That's the job.

The brand is clean, progressive and a little irreverent. Think of a well-made tool more than a consultancy: plain surfaces, one sharp color, hard numbers. It should read as the opposite of the old analyst firms: no quadrant charts, no stock photos of handshakes, no 80-page PDFs.

## Principles

- Clean, bold, and progressive

## Positioning

- **What we are:** a firm. Not a publication, not a community, never a conference company.
- **Who it's for:** Gen Z and millennial marketing and agency leaders running AI transformation at progressive, AI-native brands.
- **The enemy:** legacy analyst firms. Pay-to-play rankings, opinion dressed up as research, reports that are out of date when they ship.
- **The feeling:** relief. Finally, someone checked.
- **The reference points:** the confidence of A24 and Supreme, the clarity of Stripe and Ramp.

## Voice

Irreverent, but every line is backed by data. We earn the attitude with the evidence.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with the finding: "Human review added 3 rounds and no quality." | Lead with the method: "In this study we sought to explore…" |
| Name the number, the sample and the date. | Say "significant" or "leading" without a figure. |
| Use short declaratives. One idea per sentence. | Stack qualifiers: "may potentially help to drive". |
| Be dry and a bit funny about the industry. | Mock people or specific competitors by name. |
| Say "we tested", "we measured", "we were wrong". | Say "thought leadership", "synergy", "unlock". |

**Taglines:** Know what good looks like. · Measured, not ranked. · Check your work.

**Naming things:** playbooks are *Plumb Lines*. A workflow that meets the standard is *Plumbed*. Eval IDs are mono labels: `EVAL 014`.

## Logo

The logo has two parts. The **wordmark** is lowercase *plumb* in a custom monoline geometric drawn on a heavy 20-unit stroke. The **mark** is a plumb bob in `line-soft` hanging from a `line` purple string. Files are in Assets → Logos.

- Use the **lockup** (mark + wordmark) by default. Use the wordmark alone where the mark would be tiny. Use the mark alone for favicons, avatars and as a bullet for the single most important point on a page.
- Clear space is the height of the bob on all sides.
- The wordmark is `line` purple on white or paper, or white on purple or ink. Never recolor the bob.
- In copy, the name is written **Plumb** (capital P). The lowercase form exists only as the drawn wordmark.

### The Plumbed seal

The seal is the mark of the standard: a deep purple disc with the bob, PLUMBED and the grade and year. It goes on agency and vendor work that passes a Plumb eval. Never stretch it, recolor it, or set it on a busy image.

## Color

Plumb is purple. Clean white surfaces, one confident purple, soft lavender for whatever is selected.

- **paper / paper-raised** (near-white and white) do most of the work, with **ink** text. Both themes are first-class.
- **line** (Plumb purple) is the brand color: wordmark, primary buttons, links, active filters, data bars.
- **line-soft** is the lighter purple of the bob and big graphic fills. Never text.
- **lavender** marks what's selected or active: the current nav item, a chosen filter, a highlighted row.
- **level** means "meets the standard". Pair it with a word or a ✓, never color alone.
- **chalk** and **hairline** do the structure: tints, borders, 1px rules.

No gradients. No drop shadows.

## Type

Three families, all free on Google Fonts:

- **Geist** for everything structural: display, headlines, body. Bold (700) for display, headline and title, with tight tracking on big sizes.
- **Instrument Serif italic** for the human aside. One line at most, usually the turn in a headline: "Most AI workflows are theater. *The old way was a slide deck.*"
- **Geist Mono** for data: figures, eval labels, table numbers. If it was measured, it's in mono.

## Layout

- Soft, not boxy. Cards and tables on `radius-lg`, nav and panels on `radius-md`, controls on `radius-sm`, chips as pills.
- One strong vertical. Pages hang from a single left edge, like a plumb line. Rules are vertical or horizontal, never diagonal.
- Generous margins (`space-10`), sections `space-16` apart.
- Charts: ink and lead for everything, `line` for the one series that matters. Every chart carries a mono label with eval ID, N and date.

## Imagery

Real screens, real outputs, real people at work, shot plainly. When illustrating, use flat color blocks and vertical lines from the palette. No 3D blobs, robots, glowing brains or blue-purple "AI" gradients.
