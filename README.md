# Plumb

**The standard for AI-native marketing work: AI marketing evals for AI martech.**

> Know what good looks like. Proof, not promises. Scores can't be bought.

Plumb runs AI agents against marketing platforms through their MCP servers, makes them do real marketing-ops work at three levels of autonomy (human in, on and out of the loop), scores every run, and has practitioners grade the outcome. Results are published as the **Plumb Index**, and every result has a forkable playbook behind it, a **Plumb Line**.
