# Plumb

**The standard for AI-native marketing work: AI marketing evals for AI martech.**

> Know what good looks like. Proof, not promises. Scores can't be bought.

Plumb takes real marketing tasks, each a workflow across several tools, and runs them three ways: human in the loop, human on the loop (directing agents through MCP) and human out of the loop. It measures the time, cost and impact of each, step by step, and practitioners grade the outcome. The **Plumb Index** lists the tasks and their results; every task has a forkable playbook behind it, a **Plumb Line**.
