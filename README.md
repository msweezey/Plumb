# Plumb

**Plumb is an AI-native analyst firm.** It shows a brand how its marketing stacks up in its market, and what it takes to improve.

> Know what good looks like.

Plumb has three parts:

- **The Plumb Index**, a data product. It ranks brands within a category on how they show up where shopping is going: AI answers, search, media and agent checkout. It is published monthly, per category.
- **Peer boards.** Small, closed groups of comparable brands that benchmark against each other and compare what works.
- **AI-native playbooks.** Forkable, measured workflows (team, stack, prompts, oversight) that show how to close a gap, with outcomes Plumb has verified.
