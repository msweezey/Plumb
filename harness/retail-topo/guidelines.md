# Topo Designs — brand guidelines (guidelines.md)

Public build v0.3, 22 Nov 2026, built the same way as Pact (assumptions first, then evidence, then rules calibrated against Topo Designs' real copy; see test_report_fidelity.md). **Brand** rules describe how Topo Designs actually writes and are tuned to pass its real copy; **compliance** rules are stricter than the brand's own copy and win on conflict.

Rules: 181 (118 brand, 63 compliance). Checked automatically: 142 (78%).

## 1. Brand essence (BRD)

- Essence: outdoor gear without the gatekeeping, made for anywhere on your map.
- Voice: friendly, easygoing, crafted, playful; ads emoji-free and Title Case, social 1–3 emoji and no hashtags.
- Mechanics measured on tuning copy: ad grade median 6.8; 0 emoji in ads; 89% of IG posts use emoji; CTA 'Shop now'.
- Visual: bone #F5F4EF and charcoal #241F20 with Topo Red #E31B23; New Spirit Condensed headings, IBM Plex Sans body.
- Commerce: up to 50% off select styles, Topo Crew 10%, free U.S. shipping $100+, M.A.P. Guarantee™, many collabs.

- **BRD-01** Purpose: get everyone outside, without the gatekeeping that holds people back (co-founder Jedd Rose). _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-02** Positioning: durable, classic, functional bags and apparel designed in Colorado since 2008, for town and trail. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-03** Lead with where it goes and what it carries; materials and features are the proof. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-04** Trait 1 — Friendly: 'our friends', thank-yous, community; is not corporate. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-05** Trait 2 — Easygoing outdoors: trailheads and tailgates alike; is not hardcore, technical-only or gatekeeping. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-06** Trait 3 — Crafted: specific about materials, capacity and features; is not a spec-sheet dump. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-07** Trait 4 — Playful: light humor and a few emoji on social; is not goofy in ads. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-08** Current line is 'Made for anywhere on your map.' Never say products are 'handmade in Colorado' or 'all made in the USA' (retired, pre-2015 claims). _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_
