## Boilerplate
**Short:** Topo Designs makes bags, packs and apparel for anywhere on your map. Designed in Colorado since 2008, our gear is built to last and backed by the M.A.P. Guarantee™, from the commute to the trailhead. We like getting everyone outside, and we like doing it with friends.

**Long:** Topo Designs makes bags, packs and apparel for anywhere on your map. It started in 2008 with a sewing machine in a Fort Collins basement and a simple idea: outdoor gear like the kind we grew up using, durable, functional and classic. Today we design in Denver and make gear for commutes, road trips, flights and trailheads, much of it in recycled nylon and organic cotton. Everything we make is backed by the M.A.P. Guarantee™. We collaborate with friends we admire, from breweries to watchmakers, and we believe the outdoors belongs to everyone.
