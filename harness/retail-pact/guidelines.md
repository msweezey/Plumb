# Pact — brand guidelines (guidelines.md)

Public build v0.3, 22 Nov 2026 (calibrated against Pact's real copy; see test_report_fidelity.md). Rules come in two layers: **brand** rules describe how Pact actually writes and are tuned to pass its real copy; **compliance** rules are stricter than Pact's own copy on purpose, because catching legal and claims risk is what Plumb verifies. The build was reverse-engineered from Pact's public website, Meta ads, Instagram, Google Shopping listings and press, then completed by Plumb where the public record is silent. Each rule is tagged by provenance: **observed** (seen in Pact's own material), **inferred** (a pattern across sources) or **harness** (set by Plumb). Partner-supplied guidelines replace this build; rule IDs stay the same.

Rules: 181. Checked automatically: 142 (78%). Severity: 37 critical, 59 major, 85 minor.

## 1. Brand essence (BRD)

**Purpose:** make getting dressed an act of self-care, with clothes made to clear, certified standards.

**Positioning:** certified organic essentials, gentle on skin, priced for everyday life.

| Trait | Is | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| Caring | Warm, reassuring | Preachy, guilt-driven |
| Clear | Plain about what's in the clothes | Jargon, acronym stacks |
| Grounded | Honest about limits, specific | Absolute, perfectionist |
| Easygoing | Relaxed, a little playful | Slangy outside social, try-hard |

## 2. Voice and tone (VOI)

**Words to use (20):** organic cotton, GOTS-certified, Fair Trade Certified™, soft, breathable, everyday, essentials, clean, gentle on skin, made without, wellness, feel-good, easy, comfort, layers, standards, certified, real life, intention, restock.

**Words never to use (30):** eco-friendly, chemical-free, toxin-free, non-toxic, detox, hypoallergenic, antibacterial, cures, heals, treats, eczema, dermatologist-approved, clinically proven, plastic-free, zero waste, carbon negative, climate positive, B Corp, Earth's Favorite, cheap, flawless, guaranteed, #1, miracle, game-changer, insane, act now, don't miss out, once-in-a-lifetime, sweatshop-free. (Catalog product names are exempt, e.g. 'Perfect Rib'.)

| Context | We say | We never say |
|---|---|---|
| Promo | Everything is up to 40% off through Sunday. / Restock what you wear most. | HURRY!!! Prices this low won't last!!! / Don't miss out! |
| Service | Your order shipped today. Track it here. / We'll reply by email within one business day. | We are experiencing delays beyond our control. / Per our policy... |
| Apology | We got this wrong. A replacement is on its way. / Thanks for telling us. Here's the fix. | Due to unprecedented demand, mistakes happen. / Sorry for any inconvenience. |
| Onboarding | Welcome. Here's what certified organic means for what you wear. / Start with the basics you wear most. | Welcome! Here are 12 things about us... / You need to switch everything today. |
| Sensitive news | We've paused sales of one style while we check a fabric issue. / Here's what this means for your order. | Oops! While we sort this out, shop new arrivals! / Nothing to worry about! |

**Mechanics:** 'Pact' in running copy; serial comma optional but consistent within an asset; $10 not $10.00; 40% not 40 percent; spaced em dash for asides; grade 10 reading level or below for longer copy.

- **VOI-01** Never use any word on the banned list (30 words) in customer-facing copy. _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, harness]_
- **VOI-02** Prefer the brand vocabulary (20 words); at least one brand word per asset. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-03** Write the brand name as 'Pact' in running copy. Never 'PACT' or 'pact' in sentences. _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_ Pass: "Pact makes organic essentials.". Fail: "PACT is organic cotton.".
- **VOI-04** The spaced wordmark 'p a c t' appears only as a logo or display lockup, never in a sentence. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-05** Serial comma is optional (Pact uses both); be consistent within one asset. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_ Pass: "soft, breathable, and easy ... calm, clean, and simple". Fail: "soft, breathable, and easy ... calm, clean and simple".
- **VOI-06** Prices drop trailing zeros: $10 not $10.00; keep cents only when non-zero. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-07** Percentages as numerals with %: '40%' not '40 percent'. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-08** Use a spaced em dash ' — ' for asides; never double hyphens. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-09** No emoji in email body, web, PDP or landing page copy (subject lines may carry one). _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-10** Organic social: up to 3 emoji per caption. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-11** Paid ads: no more than 1 emoji per ad. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-12** Exclamation marks: none in ads, PDP, web and email body; one per social caption at most. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-13** Reading level: grade 10 or below (Flesch-Kincaid) for ads, email, web and PDP copy of 3+ sentences; shorter copy and social are judged, not scored. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-14** Sentences average 18 words or fewer (ads, email, web, PDP); a spaced em dash counts as a break. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-15** Speak to the reader as 'you'; refer to the brand as 'we' or 'Pact', never 'the company'. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-16** 'Made without' / 'free from' / 'no' lists name only the approved items (harsh chemicals, toxic dyes, PFAS), in any order and any subset. Never add other substances (bare 'dyes' is MSG-16). _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-17** Tone — promo: upbeat and direct about the deal, never frantic. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_ Pass: "Everything is up to 40% off through Sunday.". Fail: "HURRY!!! Prices this low won't last!!!".
- **VOI-18** Tone — service: practical, specific, owns the next step. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, harness]_ Pass: "Your order shipped today. Track it here.". Fail: "We are experiencing delays beyond our control.".
- **VOI-19** Tone — apology: brief, sincere, no excuses, one concrete remedy. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, harness]_ Pass: "We got this wrong. A replacement is on its way.". Fail: "Due to unprecedented demand, mistakes happen.".
- **VOI-20** Tone — onboarding: welcoming, explains what makes Pact different in one idea per message. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_ Pass: "Welcome. Here's what 'certified organic' means for what you wear.". Fail: "Welcome! Here are 12 things about us, our certifications, our mission...".
- **VOI-21** Tone — sensitive news (recall, price change, delay): plain facts first, no promotional lines. _[compliance layer, soft, critical, AI judge, harness]_ Pass: "We've paused sales of one style while we check a fabric issue.". Fail: "Oops! While we sort this out, shop our new arrivals!".
- **VOI-22** Social may use light internet phrases already in brand use (e.g. 'girl math', 'restock'); never slurs, body comments or slang that mocks. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, observed]_
- **VOI-23** No body-shaming or body-fixing language ('flattering for your shape', 'hides', 'slimming'). _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, harness]_
- **VOI-24** No fake familiarity or guilt ('you NEED this', 'shame on'). _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, harness]_
- **VOI-25** Sounds like Pact: warm, clear, grounded, easy (matches golden examples). _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_

- **MSG-13** Boilerplate (short, 50 words) and (long, 100 words) are used word for word from library/copy-bank.md. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, harness]_

## 10. Legal (LEG)

Core legal rules (LEG-01 to LEG-09) apply to every brand; LEG-10 to LEG-20 are the retail and Pact overlay. Critical legal rules win over any other rule (precedence). Locked text: address '4725 Walnut St, Boulder, CO 80301'; SMS 'Reply STOP to opt out'; final sale 'Final sale, not eligible for return or exchange'.

- **LEG-03** Privacy link in every email, SMS program terms and landing page footer. _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_
- **LEG-04** Trademark symbols: 'Fair Trade Certified™' with ™ on first mention. _[compliance layer, hard, minor, auto, observed]_
- **LEG-05** Every objective claim has substantiation listed in claims.md. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
