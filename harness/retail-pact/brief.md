# Pact — brand brief (brief.md)

Public build v0.1, 22 Nov 2026. Built from Pact's public website, Meta ad library, Instagram, Google Shopping listings and press (see evidence log). Each field is marked **observed** (seen directly), **inferred** (read across sources) or **harness** (set by Plumb because it isn't public). Partner-supplied guidelines replace this build when available.

| Field | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Company | Pact (Wear Pact LLC). Founded 2009, Boulder, Colorado (4725 Walnut St, Boulder, CO 80301). Private; founder-led since the 2011 Revelry Brands acquisition. 1M+ customers (2023). Revenue band: low tens of millions USD (third-party estimates disagree, $5M–$24M). Staff 35–200 by source. | observed / estimates |
| Business model | DTC site with loyalty (Pact Balance: 5% credit on orders, $3 credit per verified review). Also sold through Target, Macy's, Nordstrom, Zappos, Whole Foods and Amazon (out of scope for tests). Resale via Pact Resale and a thredUP closet clean-out. Affiliate program. Free standard shipping at $99+. | observed |
| Products | Four lines: women's apparel (dresses, leggings, tops, sets, sweaters), underwear and bras (women's and men's, multipacks), men's apparel and socks, home (sheets, duvets, quilts, towels). Prices $10 (single underwear) to $175 (quilt). Heroes: On The Go-To Legging, Fit & Flare dresses, Everyday underwear packs, Room Service Sateen sheets. New this year: matching sets (waffle, velour), Fit & Flare collection. | observed |
| Customers | Uses the shared Plumb personas (harness/shared/personas.md). Primary: **MIL** (Millennial). Secondary: **GX** (Gen X). Also reached: GZ through $10–$20 price points and creators; BB through bedding and retail partners. Women-led household buying, often for partners and kids. | inferred |
| Competitors | Big-box and mall basics (cheaper, conventional cotton); clean-label and organic DTC peers (similar claims, often pricier); department-store basics. Never named in customer-facing copy. | inferred |
| Positioning | **Certified organic essentials, gentle on skin, priced for everyday life.** | inferred |
| Value prop 1 | Gentle on skin — proof: GOTS-certified organic cotton; made without harsh chemicals, toxic dyes and PFAS. | observed |
| Value prop 2 | Verified standards — proof: GOTS certification (Control Union), Fair Trade Certified™ factories, carbon neutral through SimpliZero offsets. | observed |
| Value prop 3 | Organic for everyday life — proof: underwear from $10, leggings from $20 (during offer), free shipping at $99+. | observed |
| Tagline | Current: **Dress Yourself Well**. Retired: "Earth's Favorite™ Clothing" (still in some page metadata; never use). | observed |
| Goals this year | 1) Grow Pact Balance membership 20%. 2) Lift repeat purchase rate from 30% to 36%. 3) Make "wellness" the brand's first association in post-purchase surveys. | harness |
| Channel mix | Paid social 30% (1,257 ads in the Meta library), email 25%, organic social and creators 15%, search 15%, affiliates 10%, SMS 5%. Asset types in use: email, social, DR, DCO (catalog ads), landing pages, website; brand video light. | inferred (weights harness) |
| Calendar | Underwear Month (Aug), Fair Trade Month (Oct), Singles Day (Nov 11), Black Friday early access (Nov 17–23), Cyber week and holiday gifting, wellness events (e.g. Eudemonia Summit, Nov), spring refresh. | observed / inferred |
