# M.M.LaFleur — Method A: assumptions written before research (22 Nov 2026)

A01 Founded ~2013 in New York by Sarah LaFleur; HQ NYC. (H)
A02 DTC-led with showrooms/"styling" locations in a few cities; little or no wholesale. (M)
A03 Revenue roughly $20–100M; 100–300 staff. (L)
A04 Products: workwear for women — dresses, blazers/jackets, trousers, knit tops, sweaters; some accessories. (H)
A05 Prices: tops ~$100–200, dresses ~$200–300, jackets ~$300+. (M)
A06 Hero: machine-washable, wrinkle-resistant dresses; product names are women's first names (e.g. "The Annie"). (M)
A07 Styling service called the "Bento" box, curated by stylists. (H)
A08 Positioning: polished, versatile workwear that makes getting dressed easy for busy professional women. (H)
A09 Voice: confident, smart, a little witty; editorial; speaks to "you" as a capable professional. (M)
A10 Uses words like "effortless", "polished", "versatile", "machine washable", "travel-friendly". (M)
A11 Low emoji; few exclamation marks. (M)
A12 Sizes inclusive: 0–22 or similar. (M)
A13 Runs sales but less often than mass brands; uses "sample sale" and seasonal sales. (M)
A14 Free shipping and free returns, with a threshold. (L)
A15 Palette: black, white, muted tones; minimal; maybe a deep accent. (L)
A16 Type: elegant serif headlines with clean sans body. (L)
A17 Imagery: real professional women (not only models), city settings, offices. (M)
A18 Audience: women 30–55, managers to executives. (H)
A19 Channels: email, Instagram, Meta ads, podcasts/press; LinkedIn presence. (M)
A20 Claims: fabric performance (wrinkle-resistant, machine washable, stretch), "made in" claims for some fabrics (Italian/Japanese fabrics). (M)
A21 Sustainability is secondary; some recycled or responsible fabric claims. (L)
A22 Uses customer quotes and "as seen on" press. (L)
A23 Legal risk: fabric performance and "made in" claims; reference pricing during sales. (M)
