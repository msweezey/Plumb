# Edikted — brand guidelines (guidelines.md)

Public build v0.3, 22 Nov 2026, built the same way as Pact (assumptions first, then evidence, then rules calibrated against Edikted's real copy; see test_report_fidelity.md). **Brand** rules describe how Edikted actually writes and are tuned to pass its real copy; **compliance** rules are stricter than the brand's own copy and win on conflict.

Rules: 181 (118 brand, 63 compliance). Checked automatically: 143 (79%).

## 1. Brand essence (BRD)

- Essence: the trend from your feed, now, at a price that doesn't sting.
- Voice: loud, insider, hype, playful; emoji and caps required in ads (min 1 each), #EDIKTED on every post.
- Mechanics measured on tuning copy: ad grade median 7.7; 100% of ads have emoji; CTA always 'Shop now'.
- Visual: ink #252424 and bubblegum #EC6BAD with pastel panels; extended and wide display type.
- Commerce: weekly drops, 60% off BF (comp value), warehouse 70–80%, $80 free shipping, APP10.

- **BRD-01** Purpose: get the outfit she saw on her feed into her closet fast, at a price that doesn't sting. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-02** Positioning: trend-led going-out and everyday fits for Gen Z, most under $40, launched in weekly drops and shown on creators first. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-03** Lead with the fit or the moment (GNO, holiday party, class); price and sale come second unless it is a sale message. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-04** Trait 1 — Loud: caps-and-emoji bursts on hype words; is not one long all-caps wall. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-05** Trait 2 — Insider: talks like her group chat ('RN', 'fits', 'hits different'); is not dated slang or brand-trying-to-be-cool. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-06** Trait 3 — Hype: real urgency tied to real dates and drops; is not fake scarcity. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-07** Trait 4 — Playful and flirty: fun, confident, a wink; is not sexualized and never body-shaming. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-08** No standing tagline. Campaign line 'Totally Edikted' is BF 2026 only; never invent a tagline. _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_

- **VOI-20** Tone — onboarding: welcome to the crew, app code APP10, how drops work. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **VOI-21** Tone — sensitive news (recall, price change, delay): plain facts first, no promotional lines. _[compliance layer, soft, critical, AI judge, harness]_
- **VOI-22** Social uses current internet phrasing already in brand use ('RN', 'hits different', 'GNO', 'cozy babes'); always #EDIKTED. _[brand layer, soft, minor, auto, observed]_
- **VOI-23** No body-shaming or body-fixing language ('flattering for your shape', 'hides', 'slimming'). _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, harness]_

## 3. Messaging (MSG)



- **MSG-01** Use only claims listed in library/claims.md, worded as approved. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-02** Value prop 1 — The trend, now: weekly drops of what's on her feed. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **MSG-03** Value prop 2 — Prices that don't sting: most pieces under $40 (catalog median $26). _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **MSG-04** Value prop 3 — Seen on creators: fit shown on real creators; model size and height on every PDP. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **MSG-05** 'Starting at $X' only when an active, in-stock SKU is currently priced at $X. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-06** 'Up to X% off' only with the maximum stated in offers.csv for that offer. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-07** Never print fiber percentages; the catalog lists fibers only. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, observed]_
- **MSG-08** Items with status coming_soon are teased ('drops 12/16'), never 'shop now'. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-09** 'Everything' or 'sitewide' only when the offer has no item exclusions (OF-01 excludes warehouse items and gift cards: say 'everything*' with the exclusion line). _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-10** Savings claims carry '*Based on comp value'; never show a struck 'was' price unless msrp_comparison_allowed = yes. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-11** Never call anything sustainable, eco or ethical; Edikted makes no sustainability claims. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-12** Never name competitors (Shein, Princess Polly, Brandy Melville) in owned copy. _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-13** Boilerplate (short, 50 words) and (long, 100 words) are used word for word from library/copy-bank.md. _[brand layer, hard, minor, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-14** Name products exactly as the catalog. _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_
- **MSG-16** Prop 65: PDPs for prop65 = yes SKUs carry the CA warning line; never claim 'non-toxic'. _[compliance layer, hard, critical, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-17** No comparative or superiority claims ('better than denim', 'the leading brand') unless in claims.md. _[compliance layer, hard, major, auto, harness]_
- **MSG-15** Message fits the shared persona (GZ, MIL, GX, BB) and segment named in the task. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, harness]_
