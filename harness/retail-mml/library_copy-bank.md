## Boilerplate
**Short:** M.M.LaFleur designs workwear for women who lead. Founded in New York in 2013, we make polished, easy-care pieces, many machine washable and wrinkle-resistant, and pair them with complimentary personal styling in our showrooms and online. We call it Power Casual. Live with Purpose, Dress with Ease.

**Long:** M.M.LaFleur designs workwear for women who lead. Founded in New York in 2013 by Sarah LaFleur, with designer Miyako Nakamura, the brand makes polished pieces in fabrics that keep up with a real working day: many are machine washable, wrinkle-resistant and made with stretch. Product names carry a first name and a nickname because each piece is designed with a woman in mind. Our stylists help clients build wardrobes in our showrooms and online, at no cost. We call the result Power Casual: tailored lines, comfortable fabrics, fewer decisions. Live with Purpose, Dress with Ease.
