# M.M.LaFleur — brand guidelines (guidelines.md)

Public build v0.3, 22 Nov 2026, built the same way as Pact (assumptions first, then evidence, then rules calibrated against M.M.LaFleur's real copy; see test_report_fidelity.md). **Brand** rules describe how M.M.LaFleur actually writes and are tuned to pass its real copy; **compliance** rules are stricter than the brand's own copy and win on conflict.

Rules: 181 (118 brand, 63 compliance). Checked automatically: 142 (78%).

## 1. Brand essence (BRD)

- Essence: Power Casual for women who lead; purpose is fewer decisions and more ease.
- Voice: polished, witty, practical, assured; zero exclamation marks; editorial social devices (quotes, Samantha, 'Some decisions are easy:').
- Mechanics measured on tuning copy: ad reading grade median 5.4 (max 6.9), PDP grade up to 13; 0 hashtags; CTAs 'Shop now'/'Learn more'.
- Visual: black/white/ivory with espresso #251001; Geist sans plus a house serif.
- Commerce: BF tiers 10–40%, sale up to 70%, $400 shipping threshold (inferred), 21-day returns with an $8.95 fee.

- **BRD-01** Purpose: help women who lead get dressed with ease, so their attention goes to the work and the life, not the closet. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-02** Positioning: 'Power Casual' — polished, easy-care pieces designed in New York for real working days, with personal styling. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-03** Lead with her day (meetings, travel, dinner after); fabric performance is the proof, never the headline alone. _[brand layer, soft, major, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-04** Trait 1 — Polished: composed, precise sentences; is not stiff or corporate. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-05** Trait 2 — Witty: dry, knowing humor ('A generous spirit, technically.'); is not jokey, slangy or meme-driven. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-06** Trait 3 — Practical: specific about fabric, care, pockets and fit; is not fashion jargon. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, observed]_
- **BRD-07** Trait 4 — Assured: speaks to a capable woman as a peer; is not pushy, bossy or flattering. _[brand layer, soft, minor, AI judge, inferred]_
- **BRD-08** Current line is 'Live with Purpose, Dress with Ease' (site title). Never present the retired 'Bento Box' service as current; say 'personal styling'. _[brand layer, hard, major, auto, observed]_
